MS CODEFORGE LEGAL CENTRE

Counter-Notice and Appeals Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Policy explains how a person may challenge an MS CodeForge decision to restrict, disable or remove a resource or access to a Service.

1. Who may appeal

A person or organisation materially affected by a removal, restriction, suspension or enforcement decision may appeal, unless a legal order or safety requirement prevents review or disclosure.

2. What to submit

Provide the case reference, affected URL or product, the decision being challenged, reasons the decision is mistaken or disproportionate, supporting evidence, your authority to act and current contact details.

3. Grounds

Grounds may include mistaken identity, ownership or licence, inaccurate facts, lawful authorisation, lack of jurisdiction, disproportionate action, new evidence or completion of corrective steps.

4. Review

A reviewer should, where practicable, be different from the person who made the initial decision. We may request more information and may keep an interim restriction in place while a credible risk remains.

5. Target times

We aim to acknowledge complete appeals within five business days and target a decision within ten business days after receiving all necessary information. Complex matters, legal processes or urgent risks may take longer. These are service targets, not statutory deadlines.

6. Outcomes

We may uphold, vary or reverse the decision; restore access; maintain a restriction; request further evidence; or refer the matter for legal or security review. We will give reasons where lawful and appropriate.

7. External remedies

An internal appeal does not remove a person’s right to approach a competent regulator, tribunal or court.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
