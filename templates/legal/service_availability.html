Service Availability and Maintenance Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Policy describes the operational approach to availability, maintenance and incidents for MS CodeForge Services. A paid product may require a separate service-level agreement.

1. Availability

We aim to operate Services reliably but do not guarantee continuous or uninterrupted availability unless a separate written service-level agreement expressly provides otherwise.

2. Maintenance

We may perform planned or emergency maintenance, deploy updates, address vulnerabilities or change infrastructure. Where practicable, planned maintenance will be communicated in advance.

3. Incidents

We assess incidents based on impact, urgency, security and affected functionality. We may temporarily disable affected features to contain risk and restore safe operation.

4. Data and backups

Backup frequency, recovery objectives and retention vary by Service and must be described in product-specific documentation where relevant. Users should maintain copies of information they cannot afford to lose.

5. Third-party dependencies

Availability may depend on hosting providers, network operators, app stores, APIs or other third parties outside our direct control. We will take reasonable steps within our control to manage material dependencies.

6. Updates

We may revise this Policy as our infrastructure and products change.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
