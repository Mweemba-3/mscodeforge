MS CODEFORGE LEGAL CENTRE

Copyright and Intellectual Property Complaints

Last updated: 9 November 2026

MS CodeForge respects intellectual property rights and expects users of its Services to do the same. This Policy provides an internal route for reporting suspected infringement involving materials controlled by MS CodeForge.

1. What to include

Identify the copyrighted work, explain your ownership or authority to act, identify the exact material alleged to infringe and its location, provide contact details, explain why the use is unauthorised, and include a good-faith statement that the information is accurate.

2. Authority and evidence

Where reasonably available, provide registration or ownership records, original publication details, licence terms, assignment documents or other evidence. A registration certificate is not necessarily the only possible evidence of rights. Do not submit original confidential source code unless it is necessary and safe to do so.

3. Review

We will assess whether the material is controlled by MS CodeForge, whether the complaint is sufficiently specific and whether there may be a licence, permission, exception or other lawful basis for the use. We may request further information.

4. Action

If a complaint is substantiated or an interim restriction is justified, we may remove, disable, replace or restrict the material, or take another proportionate step. We may reject incomplete or unsupported claims.

5. Counter-notice

The affected person may submit evidence of ownership, licence, permission, mistaken identity or another lawful basis. We may restore material where appropriate after review, unless a legal restriction or other risk remains.

6. Good faith

Do not knowingly make a false ownership claim or misrepresent the location or nature of the alleged infringement. This internal procedure does not replace remedies available under the Copyright and Performance Rights Act, Chapter 406, or other applicable law.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
