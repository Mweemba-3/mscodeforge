Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MS CodeForge handles personal data when a person visits this website, contacts us, requests support or uses a Service. It is drafted with the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 of Zambia in mind. The final operator details, actual processing purposes, retention periods and systems must be verified before publication.

1. Data controller and contact

The operator responsible for a particular Service must be identified by its complete legal name and contact address. Insert the operator’s verified details and a monitored privacy contact before publication. Do not describe MS CodeForge as a registered company unless its legal status supports that description.

2. Personal data we may collect

Depending on the Service and your interaction, we may process contact details you submit, account identifiers where accounts exist, support correspondence, contact-form content, transaction information if payments are later introduced, and technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, security events and server logs.

3. Sources of data

We generally obtain data directly from you, automatically from the device or browser used to access a Service, or from a service provider that supports hosting, security, communications or another disclosed function. We do not intentionally collect more information than is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.

4. Purposes and lawful basis

We may use personal data to respond to enquiries, provide and maintain Services, authenticate administrators, protect systems, investigate abuse, comply with legal obligations and establish or defend legal claims. For each processing activity, the operator must identify and document the applicable lawful basis under the Data Protection Act, including consent where required, contractual necessity, legal obligation or another lawful basis recognised by the Act.

5. Sharing and processors

We may disclose data to service providers acting on documented instructions, professional advisers, competent public authorities where legally required, or a successor in a lawful business transfer. Service-provider contracts, confidentiality, security and any required cross-border safeguards must be assessed before data is shared.

6. International transfers

If personal data is stored or accessed outside Zambia, the operator must assess and satisfy the Data Protection Act’s requirements for international transfers and any applicable conditions or safeguards. Do not assume that a cloud provider’s standard terms alone establish compliance.

7. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of legal obligations, security, dispute resolution and recordkeeping. The operator must adopt a retention schedule for contact messages, access logs, account records, backups and legal complaints. Data should be securely deleted or anonymised when no longer required, subject to lawful preservation duties.

8. Your rights

Subject to the Act and applicable exceptions, individuals may have rights concerning access to, correction of, deletion of, objection to or other handling of their personal data. Requests should be sent to the published privacy contact. We may need to verify identity and clarify a request. We will respond within the time limits required by law.

9. Security and incidents

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks, which may include access controls, password hashing, encryption in transit, backups, logging and vulnerability management. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed absolutely secure. Where a personal data breach occurs, the operator will assess it and make notifications to the Data Protection Commissioner and affected persons where and as required by law.

10. Children

Services are not intentionally designed to collect children’s personal data unless a product expressly states otherwise. If we learn that personal data has been collected unlawfully from a child, we will assess the matter and take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law.

11. Cookies and analytics

The website may use essential session or security cookies. Any analytics, advertising or non-essential tracking must be disclosed accurately and deployed with any consent or notice required by law. See the Cookie Policy. Do not claim that analytics or advertising tools are used until they have actually been installed.

12. Changes and complaints

We may update this Policy and will change the update date. You may contact us first with a privacy concern and may also exercise rights or lodge a complaint with the competent Zambian data protection authority.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
