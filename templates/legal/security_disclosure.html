Security Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

MS CodeForge welcomes responsible reports of suspected security vulnerabilities in systems that it owns or is authorised to operate.

1. Authorised scope

Only test assets that are expressly identified by MS CodeForge as in scope or for which you have written authorisation. If no scope is published, do not assume that a domain, application, server or third-party integration is authorised for testing.

2. Safe testing

Do not access, alter, download or disclose another person’s data; disrupt services; conduct denial-of-service testing; use social engineering; install persistence; or exploit a vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue safely.

3. Report contents

Include a clear description, affected asset, reproduction steps, impact assessment, timestamps, screenshots or minimal proof, and your contact details. Redact personal data, credentials and secrets.

4. Our response

We aim to acknowledge reports within five business days and provide updates as reasonably practicable. These are targets, not guarantees. We may ask for clarification and will assess severity and remediation options.

5. Coordinated disclosure

Please allow reasonable time for investigation and remediation before public disclosure. Any coordinated disclosure arrangement must be agreed in writing. This Policy does not grant permission to conduct otherwise unauthorised testing.

6. No bounty promise

Unless a separate written programme says otherwise, no payment, reward or safe-harbour commitment is offered by this Policy.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
