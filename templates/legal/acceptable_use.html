MS CODEFORGE LEGAL CENTRE

Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Policy applies to access to MS CodeForge websites, applications, software, APIs and systems. It supplements the Terms of Service and product-specific terms.

1. Lawful use

You must comply with applicable laws, third-party rights, product instructions and contractual restrictions when using a Service.

2. Prohibited conduct

You must not attempt unauthorised access; bypass authentication; probe, scan or test systems without written authorisation; interfere with service availability; distribute malware; conduct phishing, fraud or identity impersonation; exploit vulnerabilities; misuse personal data; infringe intellectual property; or use a Service to facilitate unlawful activity.

3. No unauthorised security testing

Security testing, penetration testing, reverse engineering or vulnerability scanning is permitted only where MS CodeForge has expressly authorised it in writing or where a non-excludable legal right applies. A security researcher should use the published vulnerability disclosure channel and avoid accessing, altering or exfiltrating data.

4. No public user content feature

The Services currently do not offer general public posting or sharing by users. Support submissions are private communications and must not contain unlawful material, malware, credentials belonging to another person or unnecessary sensitive personal data.

5. Enforcement

We may investigate credible reports and take proportionate steps, including warnings, access restrictions, suspension, termination, preservation of relevant records where lawful and referral to competent authorities where required or justified by law.

6. Reporting

Report suspected misuse or security incidents through the published security or legal contact. Include the affected Service, relevant URL or identifier, dates, evidence and a safe way to contact you.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
