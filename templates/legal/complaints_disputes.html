MS CODEFORGE LEGAL CENTRE

Complaints and Dispute Resolution

Last updated: 9 November 2026

MS CodeForge aims to handle complaints fairly, consistently and in a timely manner. This Policy provides an internal route and does not restrict statutory rights.

1. Submit a complaint

Identify yourself and the affected Service, describe the issue, provide dates and relevant evidence, explain the outcome sought and provide safe contact details. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal data.

2. Acknowledgement and assessment

We aim to acknowledge complaints within five business days and may request further information. We will route the complaint to an appropriate person and assess urgency, evidence and applicable obligations.

3. Response

We aim to provide a substantive response within fifteen business days after receiving sufficient information. Complex matters may take longer; we will explain material delays where practicable. These are internal targets, not statutory deadlines.

4. Escalation

If you are dissatisfied, request an internal review and explain why. You may also approach a competent regulator, consumer body, tribunal or court where the law permits.

5. Governing law

Unless mandatory law requires otherwise, these Policies are interpreted under the laws of the Republic of Zambia. Jurisdiction and remedies remain subject to applicable law.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
