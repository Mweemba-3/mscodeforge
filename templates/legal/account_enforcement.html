MS CODEFORGE LEGAL CENTRE

Account Suspension and Termination Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Policy explains when MS CodeForge may restrict access to an account, administrator console, software product or other Service.

1. Reasons for action

Action may be taken for a material breach of terms, suspected fraud, unauthorised access, credential compromise, malware, threats to system integrity or availability, infringement, unlawful conduct, non-payment under a future paid agreement, or a binding legal requirement.

2. Proportionate response

Depending on severity and urgency, action may include a warning, request for remediation, temporary limitation, credential reset, suspension, termination or removal of a compromised resource.

3. Urgent action

We may act without prior notice where necessary to protect people, systems, data or legal interests, or where prior notice is prohibited. We will provide notice and an opportunity to respond where lawful and reasonably practicable.

4. Review

Affected account holders may request review through the published contact route. We may require identity or authority verification and may preserve relevant evidence.

5. Effects of termination

Access credentials may be disabled and associated data handled under the Privacy Policy and applicable retention duties. Termination does not extinguish accrued obligations or non-excludable legal rights.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
