Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on the MS CodeForge website. The descriptions below must be checked against the actual deployed website and its third-party scripts.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Similar technologies may store or read information on a device. They can support session management, security, preferences, analytics or advertising.

2. Essential cookies

The website may use strictly necessary cookies for functions such as administrator sessions, CSRF protection and security. These cookies support requested functionality and should not be used for unrelated tracking.

3. Optional cookies

Analytics, personalisation and advertising cookies must not be activated merely because this Policy mentions them. If introduced, the website must explain their purpose, providers, duration and how consent can be managed, and obtain consent where required by applicable law.

4. Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent login or other website functions from working.

5. Third-party technologies

Embedded media, analytics, fonts, maps or other external services may set their own cookies or collect technical data. Their use must be inventoried and disclosed before deployment.

6. Updates and contact

We will update this Policy if the website’s cookie practices change. Contact the published privacy contact with questions.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
