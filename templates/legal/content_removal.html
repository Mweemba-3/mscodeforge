MS CODEFORGE LEGAL CENTRE

Content and Resource Removal Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Policy describes how MS CodeForge receives and assesses requests to remove or restrict access to materials associated with its website, applications, software products, systems or official channels. It is a voluntary internal process and does not create a statutory notice-and-takedown safe harbour or replace any procedure required by Zambian law.

1. Scope

This process covers materials hosted, controlled, published or distributed by MS CodeForge, including website pages, product listings, images, documentation, downloadable installers, software releases, links and official communications. It does not mean MS CodeForge controls independent third-party websites, app stores, repositories or services.

2. Current publishing model

MS CodeForge currently publishes its own official website and product materials. Users do not currently have a general facility to upload, publish or share public content through MS CodeForge systems. Contact forms and support messages are private communications. If user publishing is introduced, this Policy must be reviewed before launch.

3. Grounds for a request

A request may identify alleged copyright or trademark infringement, impersonation, unlawful disclosure of personal data, privacy or confidentiality violations, malware or compromised downloads, fraudulent or deceptive material, unlawful content, court orders or another specific legal or security concern. A disagreement, criticism or negative review alone is not necessarily a valid ground for removal.

4. Information required

A request should include: (a) the requester’s full name and contact details; (b) the exact URL, product, file, page or resource; (c) a precise description of the material; (d) the legal right, law, order or policy relied on; (e) supporting evidence and the requester’s authority to act; (f) the requested action; and (g) a good-faith declaration that the information is accurate. For copyright claims, identify the original work and the allegedly infringing material. Do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive data.

5. Acknowledgement and case reference

We aim to acknowledge a sufficiently identifiable request within five business days and provide a reference number. This is an operational target, not a statutory deadline or guarantee. Incomplete requests may be returned for clarification.

6. Assessment

We assess whether the resource is controlled by MS CodeForge, whether the requester has standing or authority, whether the evidence is adequate, whether the material appears unlawful or contrary to policy, whether an exception or licence may apply, and whether removal is proportionate. We may seek clarification or independent legal advice.

7. Urgent interim restrictions

Where credible information indicates an imminent security threat, serious privacy harm, malware distribution, child-safety risk or a binding legal requirement, we may temporarily disable access while investigating. An interim restriction is not a final finding of wrongdoing.

8. Decision and action

Possible outcomes include no action, a request for more information, correction or replacement, a link restriction, temporary suspension, removal of a resource, release rollback, account or product restriction, or referral to a competent authority. We will take the least restrictive action reasonably appropriate, subject to legal duties and safety considerations.

9. Notice to affected parties

Where lawful, safe and reasonably practicable, we will notify the affected publisher, product owner or other relevant party of the request and decision, with sufficient information to understand and challenge it. We may withhold notice where prohibited by law, where it could compromise an investigation or where it creates a safety or security risk.

10. Counter-notice and appeal

A person affected by a restriction may submit a reasoned appeal with the case reference, explanation, supporting evidence and contact details. We aim to acknowledge an appeal within five business days and target a decision within ten business days after receiving complete information. These are internal targets only. We may maintain a restriction while a credible legal or security risk remains.

11. False or abusive requests

Knowingly false, misleading, harassing or bad-faith requests may be rejected. We may preserve relevant records and take appropriate lawful action. A requester may be responsible for consequences of knowingly false statements under applicable law.

12. Government and court requests

Requests from public authorities or courts are assessed for authenticity, jurisdiction, legal basis, scope and validity. We may comply with binding lawful orders and may challenge or seek clarification of requests where appropriate. We do not promise to notify affected persons where notice is prohibited or would prejudice a lawful process.

13. Evidence and retention

We may retain the request, supporting evidence, decision and relevant technical records for a period justified by legal obligations, security, audit and dispute resolution. Access is restricted and retention is governed by the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

14. No guaranteed removal

Submission of a notice does not automatically establish a violation or guarantee removal. MS CodeForge is not a court and does not make final determinations of disputed legal ownership. Parties may pursue remedies before competent authorities or courts.

15. Contact

Submit requests using the designated content-removal contact published on this website. Before launch, insert a monitored email address and, if appropriate, a postal address for formal legal notices.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
