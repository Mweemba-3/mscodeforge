MS CODEFORGE LEGAL CENTRE

Government and Law-Enforcement Requests

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Policy describes the principles MS CodeForge intends to apply when receiving formal requests from courts, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or other public authorities for information, preservation or action.

1. Verification

We seek to verify the identity and authority of the requesting body, authenticity of the request, legal instrument, jurisdiction, scope, relevant account or resource and response deadline.

2. Legal review

Where appropriate, we review whether the request is legally binding, sufficiently specific and within the authority’s powers. We may seek clarification, narrow an overbroad request or challenge it through lawful channels.

3. Disclosure

We disclose only information that we are legally required or otherwise lawfully permitted to disclose, and seek to limit disclosure to the relevant scope. We do not promise that notice will always be given.

4. Preservation

We may preserve specified records where lawfully required or reasonably necessary to comply with a valid process. Preservation is not the same as disclosure.

5. Emergency requests

Urgent requests involving imminent threats to life, serious harm or critical security risks are assessed promptly, but emergency handling does not remove the need to verify authority and applicable law.

6. Transparency and records

We keep appropriate records of requests and responses, subject to legal restrictions, confidentiality and security requirements.

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
